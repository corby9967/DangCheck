# DangCheck by 인동새우깡 🍤
Check out our new app DangCheck!

A mobile application built with Flutter, designed for families who own pets.

## Key Features
- Enables family members to communicate and share updates within the app
- Check and record daily activities such as feeding and walking the pet
- Stay connected and keep everyone informed about the pet’s care routine

## Team & Achievement
Developed by a team of five members — 2 developers, 2 designers, and 1 planner.
The project won the Grand Prize (최우수상) at the 2023 Handong Global University Software Competition.
